All four setups together (50% = no preference):

- chose the **letter B**: 62.9% [59–66]
- chose the **answer option listed last**: 66.2% [63–69]
- chose the **company listed second**: 48.1% [44–52]

Brackets are pair-bootstrap 95% intervals. Whichever is far from 50% is what drives the choice.

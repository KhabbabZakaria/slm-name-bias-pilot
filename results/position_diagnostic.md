# Position-bias diagnostic (3B)

Diagnostic run added after the main run. Same pairs, conditions, orderings and samples; the only change is that the Company B block is listed before Company A.

## Letter or slot?

| layout | listed first | listed second | chose A | chose B | chose second-listed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| a_first | A | B | 26.3% | 73.7% | 73.7% |
| b_first | B | A | 15.5% | 84.5% | 15.5% |

Across both layouts, where letter and slot are counterbalanced:

- chose the **second-listed** company: 44.6%  (k=535/1200; Wilson 95% 41.8–47.4; pair-bootstrap 95% 40.8–48.2)
- chose the **letter B**: 79.1%  (k=949/1200; Wilson 95% 76.7–81.3; pair-bootstrap 95% 73.6–84.1)

A slot bias shows as the first number far from 50% and the second near it; a letter bias shows the reverse.

## Order consistency by layout

- a_first: identified 52%; anonymised 31%; swapped 39%
- b_first: identified 13%; anonymised 47%; swapped 11%

## The three numbers

| | a_first (main run) | b_first | pooled, counterbalanced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anonymised (large-cap numbers) | 54.5% [44–64] | 51.5% [41–62] | 53.0% [46–60] |
| Identified (large-cap name) | 54.0% [42–66] | 47.5% [43–52] | 50.7% [44–58] |
| Swap-following (numbers over name) | 70.6% [64–78] | 50.0% [44–57] | 58.6% [54–63] |

Brackets are pair-bootstrap 95% intervals.

Name effect (identified − anonymised): a_first -0.5, b_first -4.0, pooled -2.3 points.
